Computational neuroscience

Soma

Transparent numerical foundations for computational neuroscience. Soma takes EEG from raw recordings to spectra, motor-imagery activity, decoding, and source models built on structural MRI, and records every result with the provenance needed to reproduce it.

It ships as a scientific Python SDK with executable examples and a local Explorer app for looking at signals, anatomy and models side by side.

Language models & biomedicine

BioResearch Agent

A multi-agent research assistant for drug-repurposing questions, such as the role of MGMT in temozolomide resistance in glioblastoma. A planner splits the question between literature, chemistry and analysis specialists, which work through five MCP servers. A verifier then checks every claim in the answer against the passage it cites, and removes or flags what isn't supported.

It runs on a free stack and is served as an async API with a semantic cache, autoscaled workers, tracing and a repeated-run evaluation suite with confidence intervals.

  • MCP
  • Hybrid retrieval

Language

Aksharam അക്ഷരം

An app for English speakers learning to read the Malayalam script, a few minutes a day. It has flashcards across six collections, spaced review, short lessons, tests, and all 109 letters with pronunciation notes and sources.

It's a fully static site with no account or backend, and your progress stays in your browser.

Bharatanatyam & machine learning

Natya Archive

An interactive archive of the 50 hastas, the hand gestures of Bharatanatyam, with flashcards and quizzes. Hold a mudra up to your webcam and a small model trained on hand landmarks guesses which one it is, entirely in your browser.

It also has full-text search over the Nāṭyaśāstra that returns the original passages, not a generated answer.
